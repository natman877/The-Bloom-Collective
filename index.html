A Note from the Founder

Dear Coca-Cola Team,

My name is Hanan Faris and I am the founder of The Bloom Collective. I am writing to invite you to partner with us on Summer Social — a one-day celebration that brings people together to enjoy desserts, shop from local businesses, and create joyful memories.

The Event

Summer Social is a one-day curated experience centered on desserts, local commerce, and community. The program will feature sweets prepared by The Bloom Collective alongside a selection of local dessert vendors, complemented by retail, photo areas, and space for attendees to gather.

Program Pillars

🍰

Sip

Dessert offerings from The Bloom Collective and participating local vendors

🛍️

Shop

A curated marketplace of local small businesses and makers

📷

Create

Designated photo areas and floral installations throughout the venue

💛

Connect

A warm social environment for attendees to spend time together

Who Will Attend

The event is primarily intended for teenagers and young adults with an interest in desserts, shopping, and new experiences. Families and general weekend attendees are also warmly welcome.

The Partnership

The Bloom Collective is seeking a sponsorship partner to help support event costs and enhance the overall experience for attendees. In exchange, Coca-Cola will receive direct visibility and meaningful engagement with a relevant, targeted audience.
