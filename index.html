A Note from the Founder

Dear Coca-Cola Team,

My name is Hanan Faris and I am the founder of The Bloom Collective. I am organizing Summer Social — a fun, one-day event that brings people together to enjoy desserts, shop from local businesses, and have a good time.

It's a day where guests can enjoy different desserts — including sweets I will be preparing myself — alongside other dessert vendors offering a variety of sweet treats. There will also be shopping, photo spots, and a beautiful space for people to spend time with friends.

The goal of this event is to support small businesses, bring people together, and create a fun experience that everyone will enjoy. I also want to help local brands reach more customers.

Coca-Cola has always stood for togetherness, refreshment, and shared happiness — values that sit at the very heart of what we are creating. I believe this partnership would be a natural and meaningful fit.

Hanan Faris
Founder & Organizer · The Bloom Collective
